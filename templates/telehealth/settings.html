Camera preview, microphone-only participation and private individual calls. Recording, transcription and screen sharing are not enabled.

Sessions open 15 minutes early and close within 15 minutes after the appointment, with a maximum room duration of 120 minutes. Availability is subject to service capacity and your subscription or trial.

Where your session is processed

Amazon Chime SDK processes audio and video in Northern Virginia, United States (AWS us-east-1). NuviaMy is administered from Spain. This release does not approve EEA clinical-data processing or records excluded by your Processing Schedule; those require a separate approved arrangement.

Your practice remains responsible for participant identity, applicable telehealth requirements, patient consent, a private setting and an alternative contact method.
