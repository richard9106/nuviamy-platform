This optional connection uses your Google email and primary calendar to synchronize appointment availability. The connection is shared with your practice.

Before you connect Google Data use & privacy

This optional connection identifies your Google account by email and stores encrypted access tokens to synchronize your calendar. Authorized members of your practice can use the connection; it is shared with your practice.

Calendar access reads upcoming event titles and times from your primary calendar and synchronizes generic appointment occupancy blocks. Google and our hosting provider process the information needed to provide this feature. This connection does not request Gmail or Drive access.

We follow Google's Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google data, use it for advertising or use it to train generalized AI models. Disconnecting clears active credentials and stops the connection; it does not revoke Google's authorization or delete sent emails, external events/files or retained backups.
