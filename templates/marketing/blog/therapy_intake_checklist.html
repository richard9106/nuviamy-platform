When the first appointment is booked

Send a confirmation with the appointment date, time and time zone, location or instructions for joining, and a way to contact the practice. If you send forms separately, tell the client where to find them and when you would like them returned.

Keep the message short enough to scan on a phone. Use your practice’s approved channel for collecting sensitive information.

Before the first session

  • Check that the invitation went to the right address and that the client can open it.
  • Review which forms are complete, which are still waiting for the client, and which are waiting for staff review.
  • Send a short reminder if anything is missing. Give the client a way to ask for help or request another format.
  • Confirm that the client has the right instructions for an in-person or online appointment.

Example reminder: “Hi, this is a reminder to complete your intake forms in the client portal before your appointment on Tuesday. If you have trouble opening the portal, please contact the practice so we can help.”

Make it easy to hand off

Agree on who sends each invitation and who checks responses. Use a short set of statuses such as sent, waiting for client, ready to review, and reviewed. Then run the checklist once with a fictional client and see whether another team member can tell what needs attention without asking you.
