## Processing scope
On Customer instructions, NuviaMy collects, stores, organizes, displays to authorized users, signs, amends, exports, backs up, restores and deletes ordinary practice records. Data subjects are clients, their representatives, emergency contacts and practice personnel. Data includes contact details, appointments, intake responses, health history, diagnoses, treatment plans, ordinary progress notes and signatures, documents, invoices and security records. The Customer selects relevant data through its authorized workspace actions. Real client records within this scope may be used during the trial under these agreements.

Separately maintained HIPAA psychotherapy notes and records governed by 42 CFR Part 2 are excluded without an additional written arrangement and suitable controls. For minors, the Customer must establish lawful representative authority and appropriate access restrictions before entering their data. NuviaMy does not provide medical advice, emergency care, patient consent or a government compliance certification. Customer clinical data is not sold, used for advertising or used for independent AI training.

## Infrastructure and external services
The production application and PostgreSQL database run on Amazon EC2 with encrypted EBS storage in us-east-1 (Northern Virginia, United States). Private documents, encrypted database backups and the audit log destination use separate Amazon S3 buckets in that region. EC2 snapshots support recovery. AWS CloudTrail records infrastructure activity across regions, including global service events; IAM and CloudFormation support access and infrastructure administration. These are not promises that every service control-plane operation or support activity remains in one region.

The AWS account identifies Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL as its contractual service provider. The applicable AWS Data Processing Addendum also identifies Amazon Web Services, Inc. and the AWS contracting party or parties. AWS is the infrastructure subprocessor; its service-specific subprocessor list applies to the services and locations used. The AWS Business Associate Addendum is active for the production account, subject to eligible-service and configuration conditions. Administration by NuviaMy is from Spain. This is neither an EU-only nor a US-only access commitment.

Amazon SES in us-east-1 sends account authentication, invitation, recovery and service communications using encrypted API submission and required delivery TLS. Message destinations, account/service content and temporary authentication links are processed for delivery. No clinical notes, diagnoses or patient attachments are included in those communications. Delivery necessarily passes to the recipient's email provider; the recipient selects and secures their mailbox. This infrastructure description does not authorize sending clinical records to a personal mailbox.

Bounce and complaint metadata goes to an encrypted Amazon SNS topic and encrypted Amazon SQS queue in us-east-1. AWS KMS protects the SNS topic. This private queue retains messages for at most 14 days unless reviewed earlier; evidence requiring longer retention is separately protected under incident instructions. Original email headers are excluded, and SES feedback forwarding by email is disabled. Operational monitoring checks only queue counts and sends generic failure codes to the operator, without recipient lists or clinical details. The AWS feedback channel is not an SMS or external email subscription.

Cloudflare provides DNS with DNS-only production routing. Cloudflare Email Routing and the operator's Gmail inbox remain administrative/support channels, outside the approved clinical-content scope. Brevo is no longer the active application mail transport; any historical material requires restricted review and authorized disposition. General support must not contain clinical records, passwords or authentication codes.

Google Calendar is optional and limited to the connected user's owned calendar. Exported appointment blocks use 'Reserved time', without patient names, attendees, location, notes or clinical links. Event times and account identifiers may still be personal data; the Customer must evaluate the exact Google account, service and instructions. OAuth approval does not constitute a Google BAA or approve a clinical transfer. Gmail and Drive capabilities are disabled and are not offered by this Schedule.

Sentry, if enabled, receives sanitized structural technical diagnostics, excluding clinical content, user identities, request URLs, request bodies, breadcrumbs and local variables. Provider-side historical data and retention require separate review. Stripe handles hosted SaaS subscription payments and commercial billing data; card details are entered on its hosted page. Patient payments remain a separate assessed flow and are not approved merely by this subscription-processing description. Do not put diagnoses or clinical notes in payment metadata.

Changes of clinical subprocessors follow the DPA notice and objection procedure. A provider name alone does not establish contractual coverage for additional flows. Legacy infrastructure is not an authorized destination for new clinical records.

## EEA data and transfers
For practice records the Customer normally acts as controller and NuviaMy as processor, unless the Customer has documented another lawful role. NuviaMy's own account administration and subscription billing purposes are addressed separately in its privacy notice. The Customer supplies the applicable Article 6 basis and Article 9 health-data condition, patient notices and any required authorizations; acceptance of this Schedule is not patient consent.

Customer data is hosted in the United States as described above, with remote administration from Spain. The AWS Service Terms incorporate the AWS DPA, its Supplementary Addendum and transfer clauses subject to their conditions. For clinical records processed on behalf of the Customer, the processor-to-processor module is the relevant SCC module where SCCs apply; for NuviaMy's own controller processing, the controller-to-processor module may apply. The actual transfer instrument and any alternative mechanism must be recorded for the exact entities, data and flow. This Schedule is not itself a set of SCCs and does not select an adequacy mechanism merely because an AWS entity appears on a certification list.

The EU-U.S. DPF can cover health-related personal data when the recipient certification covers the data; it is not a separate health-service certification. AWS Inc. is listed as a covered entity in Amazon's active non-HR certification. This Schedule does not treat that listing alone as conclusive coverage of all hosted Customer Data or extend AWS's account-information notice to patient records. The transfer assessment distinguishes hosted records and backups, private support, logs, transactional messages, delivery feedback and the recipient's mailbox. Patient communications may reveal a care relationship even when their body is generic. This assessment does not establish transfer coverage for employment-context HR or occupational-health files.

Before approving an EEA clinical scope, NuviaMy must document the applicable transfer mechanism, any required transfer assessment and supplementary measures, and the Customer's authorized instructions. Acceptance of this Schedule does not record that assessment as complete or authorize a transfer without the applicable safeguards. Encryption at rest, TLS, encrypted tokens and an AWS BAA do not establish that all processing is end-to-end encrypted or that keys are exclusively held outside the hosting environment. A move to an EU region requires a separately validated change and does not follow automatically from accepting this text.

The Customer identifies countries, autonomous communities or states, professional obligations and adult/minor scope. Health-record retention is determined for those circumstances, not by a single HIPAA or EU-wide number. Patient rights are handled under the DPA, with authenticated authority, applicable legal limits and preservation of signed originals through amendments. NuviaMy assists risk and impact assessments; no EIPD or DPO appointment is represented as completed by this Schedule.

## Safeguards and assistance
Controls include HTTPS, practice-scoped authorization, protected audit evidence, encrypted integration tokens, session expiration/revocation, email two-step sign-in, mandatory authenticator enrollment for platform staff and superusers, immutable signed notes with linked addenda, encrypted EC2 storage, private encrypted/versioned S3 storage, encrypted database backups and daily encrypted instance snapshots. The Customer manages workforce permissions and device security. No certification, guaranteed uptime or recovery time is promised. NuviaMy remains responsible for appropriate safeguards and downstream obligations under the DPA and applicable BAA.

Incident reporting and cooperation follow the DPA and BAA. Initial reporting does not wait for a complete investigation. Individual requests go to the verified Customer; NuviaMy assists authorized requests promptly enough for applicable deadlines. Authorized exports include JSON records, available files and signature information; PDF summaries are not the complete archive.

## Expiry, return and deletion
Trial expiry and subscription cancellation do not automatically delete data. Authorized reading and export remain available while creation and edits are restricted by subscription status. There is no automatic charge at trial expiry, no universal medical-record retention period and no promise of indefinite storage. The Customer identifies its professional, state, age-related and legal-hold obligations.

The Customer may request return or deletion through an authenticated owner request. NuviaMy verifies authority, offers a complete export and agrees a documented closure timetable before erasure, taking account of lawful holds. No unilateral routine deletion starts merely because a trial ends. If return/destruction is infeasible, safeguards continue and uses are limited to the reason for retention under the DPA/BAA. Completion or lawful restricted retention is documented.

Current backup lifecycle: current S3 database backups expire after 35 days; noncurrent backup versions expire 35 days after becoming noncurrent, so this is not a maximum total age. Instance snapshots retain seven daily recovery points. Document versions have no automatic deletion lifecycle and must be included in verified closure instructions. Deletion requests must cover active copies, versions, snapshots and prevention of reintroduction on restore; a backup expiry guarantee is not implied by these operational settings.
